Strogonoff Recipe

Stroganoff Dish

Stroganoff, or estrogonofe as it is known in Brazil, is a creamy and savory dish made with tender pieces of chicken cooked in a rich sauce of cream, tomato, and seasonings. It’s a comforting and flavorful recipe that balances the tanginess of tomato with the smoothness of cream, creating a deliciously indulgent texture.

In Brazil, stroganoff is typically served with white rice and crispy potato sticks (batata palha), adding a crunchy contrast to the creamy sauce. Its simplicity and bold flavor make it a popular choice for everyday meals and special occasions alike.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast, cut into cubes
  • 1 tablespoon butter or oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon mustard
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup sliced mushrooms (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large pan, heat the butter or oil over medium heat. Add the chicken pieces and cook until browned and cooked through.
  2. Add the chopped onion and garlic, sautéing until soft and fragrant.
  3. If using mushrooms, add them to the pan and cook until tender.
  4. Stir in the ketchup and mustard, mixing well to coat the chicken.
  5. Reduce the heat and add the heavy cream, stirring until the sauce is smooth and heated through. Do not let it boil.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste, then serve hot.
