Fricassê Recipe

Fricasse Dish

Fricassê is a creamy and comforting dish popular in Brazil, typically made with shredded chicken, corn, and a rich, velvety sauce. The dish combines savory and slightly sweet flavors, thanks to the blend of cream, corn, and seasonings, creating a smooth and indulgent texture that coats the chicken perfectly.

Usually baked and topped with crispy potato sticks or melted cheese, fricassê offers a satisfying contrast between creamy and crunchy elements. It’s a simple yet delicious recipe, ideal for family meals or gatherings, and highlights how everyday ingredients can come together to create something hearty and flavorful.

Ingredients

  • 500g cooked and shredded chicken breast
  • 1 can of corn (drained)
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup cream cheese (requeijão)
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Potato sticks (batata palha) for topping
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a blender, combine the corn, heavy cream, cream cheese, and chicken broth. Blend until smooth and creamy. Set aside.
  2. In a pan, sauté the chopped onion and garlic until fragrant and translucent.
  3. Add the shredded chicken to the pan, season with salt and pepper, and mix well.
  4. Pour the blended cream mixture into the pan and stir until everything is well combined and heated through.
  5. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish, top with shredded mozzarella cheese, and bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) until the cheese is melted and slightly golden.
  6. Remove from the oven and top with potato sticks before serving.
